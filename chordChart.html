Chord Chart Formatter

Paste the Planning Center chord chart markup, pick a key and how chords are shown, and print a page the way you want it on your music stand.

1 · Get the chart from Planning Center

  1. Drag Get chord chart up to your bookmarks bar. You only do this once.
  2. In Planning Center Services, open a plan (or a song's arrangement) and click that bookmark. If the plan has several songs it asks which one. A green message at the top of the page confirms the chart was copied.
  3. Come back here and paste it into the box below (Ctrl+V), or press “Paste from clipboard”.
Can't drag it?

Make a new bookmark in your browser, give it any name, and paste this code as its address (URL).
