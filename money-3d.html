How big is a pile of money?

Every bill is a $20 bill, 156 × 66 mm and 0.11 mm thick, stacked flat. Fly around: drag to look, WASD to move, E/Q up/down, Shift for boost, mouse wheel to change speed. Fly right up to the big blocks to see the individual bills in the edges. In a VR headset browser an Enter VR button appears.
